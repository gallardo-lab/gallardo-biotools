Lo único que manda algo al NCBI en este bloque son los tres botones. Cambiar de gen o de transcrito no manda nada: se dibuja con lo que ya se trajo. Traer genes y transcritos preselecciona el canónico del gen y trae su proteína en esa misma pulsación.

Contesta primero y pulsa Comprobar después. Si una pregunta dice que no se puede comprobar todavía, no es que esté mal: es que falta un paso en otra pestaña, y ahí mismo te dice cuál. Tus respuestas se guardan en este navegador y entran en el reporte de la pestaña siguiente.

Reúne en un solo documento lo que entró, lo que salió y lo que contestaste, y lo sella con SHA-256. Se abre en una ventana aparte para imprimirlo a PDF.

Guardar la página con el navegador no sirve de evidencia: conserva los resultados y pierde la secuencia, el rsID, los alelos y la posición. Por eso existe esta pestaña.

How it works / Cómo funciona

Tm. Nearest-neighbor method of SantaLucia 1998 with monovalent/Mg²⁺ salt correction of Owczarzy 2004 (Mg²⁺ chelated by dNTPs is discounted). Valid for oligonucleotides; matches Primer3/Primer-BLAST within the usual range.

Search regions. This is what distinguishes the expert version: forward and reverse are constrained to independent windows (from – to, 1-based). Use it when the template has fixed anchors: a known exon, a conserved region, or a sequence you must not prime on. Leave empty to search the whole template.

Sanger readability. If a variant position is given, the pair must place it inside the amplicon and at least the minimum distance from each primer (default 60 nt), because the first 40 to 60 nt after the primer fall in the noisy region of the electropherogram. Distances FWD→SNP and SNP→REV are reported per pair.

Annealing temperature (Ta). The optimal Ta of Rychlik 1990 is reported: 0.3·Tm(least stable primer) + 0.7·Tm(product) − 14.9, using the nearest-neighbor Tm of the primer and the product Tm (81.5 + 16.6·log₁₀[Na⁺] + 0.41·%GC − 675/N). It is more reliable than the plain «Tm − 5» rule, especially with long amplicons or genomic DNA; the authors reported agreement with experimental Ta within 0.7 °C. The −5 rule is shown as a reference only.

IUPAC. Degenerate bases are accepted in the template, flagged in amber, but primers are never designed over degenerate positions.

Variant position (v1.5). If the FASTA header carries SNP_pos=N, 1-based, the position is read on load, the Min SNP dist filter becomes active and the Dist FWD→SNP and Dist SNP→REV columns are populated; the PCR in silico field is prefilled when empty, and since v2.0 also from the position you type in the RFLP tab, but only when the sequence there was brought from the design tab, because otherwise the offset is unknown. Headers written by snp_template_ensembl.py, which masks co-located variants as IUPAC using Ensembl REST, already carry it. Without such a header the field can still be typed by hand in the PCR in silico tab. In v1.4 and earlier this position was never set from the sequence, so the filter did not act.

Privacy. Design, thermodynamics and in silico PCR run entirely in your browser, and the oligo database lives in this browser's IndexedDB and is never uploaded. This page loads no external fonts or libraries. No request is made when the page loads: every outbound request is triggered by a button you press. They reach three places:

  • Annotation servers. Since 18 November 2026 no button reaches Ensembl any more: the traffic is split across three hosts, and which one a button reaches was derived from the call graph of this very file, not written from memory. (1) eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov: eight buttons. NCBI, United States. Fetch template sends the rsID you typed plus the masking set and the flank you chose; Identify and the two Fetch buttons of the RFLP tab send an rsID; Fetch CDS and Fetch genes and transcripts send a region; Fetch the protein sends a protein accession; the consequence button sends an rsID. (2) api.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov: two buttons. NCBI Datasets, United States. Fetch CDS and Fetch genes and transcripts ask it for the transcripts, their exons and their CDS. (3) myvariant.info: one button. Scripps Research, United States. The consequence button asks dbNSFP for SIFT and PolyPhen, and only when you have set an alternative allele: it sends the position and the two allele bases. None of the eight runs on its own. No sequence you paste and no primer you design is ever sent: the traffic goes one way, an identifier, a position or a region out and public reference data back. Your IP address reaches NCBI or Scripps Research, depending on the button, as with any web request, and is subject to each one's privacy notice. NCBI privacy notice EMBL-EBI privacy notice MyVariant.info terms
  • Primer-BLAST links. Clicking one opens NCBI in a new tab with the primer sequences in the URL.

If a variant identifier is itself sensitive in your setting, for example one tied to a patient or to an unpublished cohort, do not press Fetch: the identifier leaves this machine. Use the offline route instead, described below.

Offline and batch route. The companion script snp_template_ensembl.py does the same job from the terminal, in batch, and writes the FASTA together with a traceability table of every masked variant. Use it when you need reproducibility for a Methods section, many variants at once, or a record of exactly what was masked.

Not included. This tool does not do qPCR design, probe design or cloning. Those options are absent, not hidden: do not assume defaults cover them.

Support tool. Always verify specificity in Primer-BLAST; links are provided per primer and per pair.
